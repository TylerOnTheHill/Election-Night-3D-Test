# Collin County 2026 voting-precinct boundary adoption: documentary evidence

Research date: October 27, 2026.

## Confirmed county adoption

**Collin County Commissioners Court Order No. 2025-1013-09-22**, agenda item **AI-58650**, approved September 22, 2025, addresses voting-precinct boundary changes to maintain compliance with Texas Election Code §42.006.

- Official court minutes: https://eagenda.collincountytx.gov/docs/2025/CC/20250922_3137/3177_Commissioners%20Court%20Minutes%2009-22-2025.pdf
- County published adoption notice (changes effective January 1, 2026): https://www.collincountytx.gov/Elections/PublishingImages/Lists/DeptNews/AllItems/NOTICE%20OF%20BOUNDARY%20CHANGES%20TO%20VOTING%20PRECINCTS.pdf
- County precinct splits, combinations and redistricting summary (273 total): https://www.collincountytx.gov/Elections/PublishingImages/Lists/DeptNews/AllItems/2025%20Precinct%20Combinations%20Splits%20and%20Redistricting%20Summary_20250909.pdf
- Elections administrator's September 22 memo: https://eagenda.collincountytx.gov/docs/2025/CC/20250922_3137/58650_Department%20Memo-Boundary%20Changes%20to%20Voting%20Precincts.pdf

This is **documented adoption of the January 2026 precinct boundary changes**. It is **not yet a verified geometric match** between the county-adopted boundaries and the Texas Legislative Council's 2026 **primary** shapefile, nor does it prove no subsequent amendments before the November 3, 2026 general election.

## Independent official filing requirement

Texas Election Code §42.037 requires a map of the affected precincts to be filed with the Secretary of State within 120 days after the boundary-change order. Texas SOS guidance: https://www.sos.state.tx.us/elections/laws/advisory2021-14.shtml

Locate publicly accessible **maps filed pursuant to §42.037 for Court Order 2025-1013-09-22**, and check published Collin County records for whether any subsequent precinct boundary changes were adopted for the November 3, 2026 general election. A publicly accessible dated county general-election precinct GIS layer, or a traceable reputable third-party November 2026 boundary source, can support the map with its actual provider and vintage labeled. No outreach or records requests.
